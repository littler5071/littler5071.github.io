第 159-161 題

Memorandum MEMORANDUM To: Sales Representatives From: Priya Raman, Regional Manager Re: Changes to the client entertainment budget Effective 1 July, the following rules apply to expenses for entertaining clients: 1. Meals. The limit for a single client meal is NT$2,400 per person, unchanged from last year. Receipts must list every attendee by name. 2. Gifts. The annual limit per client has been reduced from NT$6,000 to NT$4,000. Gifts above NT$1,500 require my approval in writing before they are purchased. 3. Sporting and cultural events. Tickets may be purchased for clients only when a representative attends the event with them. 4. Reporting. Claims must be submitted within 14 days of the event. Claims submitted later will be paid in the following quarter. The company card may no longer be used for entertainment expenses; please pay personally and request reimbursement.

中文大意：區域經理 Priya 的備忘錄：7/1 起客戶招待費用新規——餐費每人上限 2,400 元（不變）且收據要列所有出席者姓名；年度禮品上限由 6,000 降到 4,000，超過 1,500 需事先書面核准；運動與藝文票券必須有業務代表陪同；14 天內提交請款（逾期順延一季）；不得再用公司卡，改先自付再請款。
