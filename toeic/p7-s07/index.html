Information CONFERENCE ROOM BOOKING — NORTHBRIDGE BUILDING, 8TH FLOOR Rooms A and B may be booked by any employee through the online calendar. Room C is reserved for meetings with clients and must be requested through the reception desk. Rules for use: • Bookings are limited to two hours. Meetings that need more time must be approved by a department head before the booking is made. • Cancel your booking at least 15 minutes in advance. Employees who fail to cancel three times in one quarter will lose booking privileges for the following month. • Equipment such as projectors and speakerphones is kept at the reception desk. Sign the equipment out and return it immediately after the meeting. • Rooms are cleaned every evening. Please leave the room tidy, but do not move furniture between rooms. For technical problems during a meeting, call extension 4100.
