第 150-152 題

Notice NOTICE TO ALL DEPARTMENTS — OFFICE RELOCATION Starting 1 September, the Finance and Legal departments will move from the fourth floor to the newly renovated eighth floor. The move affects 62 employees. What you need to know: • Movers will pack and transport labeled boxes on 29 and 30 August. Please put anything you want moved into a box and attach a blue label with your department name. Items without a label will not be moved. • Desks, chairs and filing cabinets are the property of the building and will stay in place. • Personal items such as plants and framed pictures should be taken home by 28 August. The eighth floor has two meeting rooms and a small kitchen. Printing services will remain on the fourth floor for the time being, so please allow extra time when you need to print large documents. Questions about the move should be sent to facilities@northbridge-example.com.
