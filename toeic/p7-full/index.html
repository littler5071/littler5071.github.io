第 156–158 題　第 4 組・公司刊物・3 題

Questions 156–158 refer to the following article.
Article

FROM THE COMPANY NEWSLETTER — WORKING FROM HOME, ONE YEAR LATER

 

When Haverton Logistics first allowed its customer service team to work from home, managers expected a drop in productivity. The opposite happened. In the first year, the average time needed to answer a customer inquiry fell from 14 hours to 9 hours, and employee turnover in that team dropped by nearly a third.

 

The company credits two decisions. First, it gave every remote employee a headset and a second monitor, which cost far less than the rent of the office space it later gave up. Second, it replaced daily check-in meetings with a written summary that each employee posts once a week, leaving managers free to deal with unusual cases.

 

Not everything worked. A pilot program that let staff choose their own working hours was discontinued after six months, because customers could not reach anyone early in the morning. Beginning in October, remote staff will follow fixed shifts again.
